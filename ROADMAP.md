| NOTES-1 | QUESTION, not a plan: is an AI-powered note-taking and organizing tool worth making at all? Answer given by s1-chat (not owner-set): not now. The category is full, the single goal is paid pilots, and people do not move their notes. The only reasons that would justify it are a front door where a person sees SmartMemory work, and our own daily use. The cheap route to both is to go where notes already live: an Obsidian plugin, or a "connect to SmartMemory" switch in Marky. Revisit after the pilots; if the demo-surface need becomes real earlier, do the plugin. | PARKED | none yet |
